# The Great Guilds: guild halls - design

2026-10-04. Status: **design, awaiting the author's review. Nothing is built.** The brainstorm
that produced it is `docs/sessions/HANDOFF_20261004_GUILDS_DOCS_PUSH_AND_HALLS_BRAINSTORM.md` §5.
Every engine fact below was read from CA's `db.pack` with `tools/read_vanilla_db.py`, or from
CA's script docs with `tools/check_lua_api.py --explain`, on 2026-10-04. Where a fact is an
inference rather than a reading, it says so and is listed in §10.

## 1. What and why

The guilds today are faction-wide reputation with no presence on the map. This adds
**guild halls**: real buildings, one chain per guild per race, that a faction unlocks by
rank. Each hall pays its guild reputation, gives a local bonus themed to the guild, makes its
guild's services cheaper, and trains one of the race's existing units. The model is Medieval 2's
guild houses: one guild per settlement, a Master Guild once per faction, and a Headquarters
reserved for the guild's leader.

Decisions taken by the author during the brainstorm:

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Role | A hall tied to rank; your rank with a guild unlocks each level |
| Placement | One guild per settlement, in an ordinary building slot |
| Tiers | 3 levels: Lodge at Indebted (any settlement), Hall at Favoured (one per guild per faction), Seat at Exalted and only while leading the guild |
| Benefits | Reputation each turn, a local themed bonus, cheaper services, a recruitable unit |
| Rank or lead lost | Built halls stay and keep working; you cannot build or upgrade more. The Seat's extra bundle works only while you lead |
| Units | Existing units of the race, no new unit keys |
| Approach | Inside `derpy_great_guilds.pack`, generated by `gen_great_guilds.py`; Chaos Dwarfs end to end first, live-checked, then the other seven races as data |

**Not in scope:** new unit keys; a guild switching an existing hall to another guild (possible
later through cross-chain upgrade edges, see §11); halls for races the mod does not cover;
destroying or downgrading halls by script.

## 3. DB shape

Keys are lowercase (the 6.1 rule). `<tag>` is the flavour tag: empty for Chaos Dwarfs,
`_emp`, `_dwf`, `_brt`, `_cth`, `_ksl`, `_def`, `_hef`.

| Thing | Key |
|---|---|
| Superchain, one per race | `derpy_gg_hall<tag>` |
| Chain, six per race | `derpy_gg_hall_<guild><tag>` |
| Level | `derpy_gg_hall_<guild>_<n><tag>`, n = 0, 1, 2 |

18 levels per race, 144 in all.

### 3.1 Tables, per race

The set comes from `Modding Files/templates/README.md` "building/". **Each of the four
placement tables is required.** Omit one and the chain never appears, with no error.

| Table | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `building_superchains` | 1 | the race's superchain |
| `building_chains` | 6 | `building_superchain` = the race's superchain; other columns cloned from the donor chain |
| `building_levels` | 18 | §5's cost, time and settlement level; `faction_unique` true on levels 1 and 2 |
| `building_upgrades_junction` | 12 | 0 to 1 and 1 to 2, per chain. No row means level 0 builds and nothing upgrades (memory `wh3-building-upgrades-junction-is-the-upgrade-edge`) |
| `building_culture_variants` | 18 | name, description, icon, culture |
| `building_chain_set_items` | 12 | each chain in `wh3_main_secondary_core_generic_minor` and `_major`, `remove` false, as the donor does |
| `building_set_to_building_junctions` | 6 | the race's own Guild Halls set (§3.2) |
| `building_chain_availability_sets` | 6 | the race's set (§3.2) |
| `building_instances` | 7 | **the superchain at 1**: one guild per settlement. Each chain at 1 |
| `building_effects_junction` | 36+ | the local bonus (§8) and the existing card line `derpy_gg_built_<guild><tag>` |
| `building_units_allowed` | 6+ | one unit per chain, with `XP` set by level (§8) |
| `cai_construction_system_building_values` | 6 | §6 |
| `effect_bundles` + `effect_bundles_to_effects_junctions` | 6 + 6 | the Seat bundles `derpy_gg_seat_<guild><tag>` (§4.5), each with its `effect_bundles_localised_title_*` / `_description_*` loc (memory: a bundle without loc draws with no title) |
| loc | per level | `building_culture_variants_name_*` / `_description_*`, the lock tooltips (§4.1), Help (§7) |

**The cap is read from each LEVEL's `building_instance_key`, not from the superchain.**
Corrected 2026-10-04 during Task 1, read from `db.pack`. Levels that share an instance key share
the cap that key's `building_instances` row sets. Cathay's yin and yang tiger walls are two
chains whose six levels all carry `building_instance_key = wh3_main_cth_defence`, and that key
has a row at 1. Their superchain, `wh3_main_cth_walls_major`, has no instances row at all.

So all 18 hall levels carry `building_instance_key = derpy_gg_hall`, and `building_instances`
has `derpy_gg_hall` at 1. An empty instance key would leave the levels uncapped: 119 vanilla
levels have one. The superchain stays shared for grouping.

## 4. Script (`zzz_derpy_guilds.lua`, a new HALLS block)

### 4.1 Locks: `GG.lock_halls(faction, guild)`

What a faction may build:

| Level | Open when |
|---|---|
| 0 | rank >= 2 (Indebted) |
| 1 | rank >= 4 (Favoured) |
| 2 | rank >= 5 (Exalted) **and** `GG.leader_of(guild, culture) == faction` |

A level that should be shut gets
`cm:add_event_restricted_building_record_for_faction(level_key, faction, tooltip_key)`, and one
that should be open gets `cm:remove_event_restricted_building_record_for_faction`. CA's doc:
the restriction "is saved into the campaign save file ... and automatically re-established when
the campaign is reloaded". The tooltip keys are `campaign_localised_strings`, one per race and
reason: "Needs Indebted with {guild}", "Needs Favoured with {guild}", "Only the leader of
{guild} may raise it".

`GG.halls_locked[faction][level_key]` remembers what was last written, so a sweep calls the
engine only for a level whose answer changed. It is session memory: empty after a load, so the
first sweep of a session writes every level once.

Called from:

- `GG.apply_rank`, for that guild;
- `GG.reassert_leaders`, for both the old and the new leader of a guild that changed hands;
- the first tick of a session, for every covered faction.

AI factions are locked by the same rules. When the MCT switch `guild_halls` is off, every level
is shut for every faction and nothing else in this block runs.

### 4.2 Counting: `GG.count_halls(faction)`

At a faction's turn start, before anything that reads it: walk `faction:region_list()`, and
for each region try the race's 18 level keys with `region:building_exists(key)`, highest level
first, stopping at the first hit (a settlement holds one hall, §3.1). The result is
`GG.halls[faction][guild] = {n = count, best = highest level}`. It is never saved: the map is
the record, so a reload cannot desync it.

### 4.3 Reputation

At turn start, each hall pays its guild `HALL_REP[level] * hall_rep / 100` through
`GG.capped_grant`, ledger source `halls`. The turn limit, the rival's share and the patron all
apply. On `BuildingCompleted`, a chain beginning `derpy_gg_hall_` maps straight to its guild,
ahead of `GG.BUILDING_THEME`'s token match, and pays the existing building rate. The faction is
read from `context:building():faction()`, never `context:faction()` (memory
`wh3-buildingcompleted-has-no-faction`).

### 4.4 Discount

`GG.service_cost` gains one term before its existing clamp:
`mod = mod - min(n * hall_off, HALL_OFF_MAX)`, where `n` is the faction's hall count for that
service's guild. The existing `FAVOUR_MIN_MOD` / `FAVOUR_MAX_MOD` clamp (-30 / +30) still bounds
the total. The price tooltip gains a "Halls" line.

### 4.5 Seat bundle

`derpy_gg_seat_<guild><tag>`, faction scope, indefinite (`-1`). It is applied while the faction
both leads the guild **and** owns a level-2 hall of it. It is swept in `GG.reassert_leaders`, and
re-checked after counting at turn start, because a captured Seat ends it. It never touches the
building's own DB effects, which stay with whoever holds the building.

### 4.6 Multiplayer

Everything above is model code, run at turn start or on a model event, identically on every
machine. No `UITrigger` messages are added. The UI only reads.

## 5. Numbers

Starting shapes, not measurements; see §10.

| | Level 0 | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rank | Indebted (100) | Favoured (700) | Exalted (1500) + leader |
| `primary_slot_building_building_level_requirement` | 2 | 3 | 4 (minors stop at 3, so the Seat needs a major settlement) |
| `create_cost` / `create_time` | 1500 / 3 | 3000 / 5 | 6000 / 8 |
| `upkeep_cost` | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `faction_unique` | false | true | true |
| `HALL_REP` per turn | 4 | 8 | 15 |
| Unit `XP` | 0 | 1 | 2 |

| Constant | Default | MCT |
|---|---|---|
| `hall_rep` | 100 (%) | Custom slider, 0-300 |
| `hall_off` | 3 (% per hall) | Custom slider, 0-10 |
| `HALL_OFF_MAX` | 15 (%) | fixed |
| Seat bundle | the hall's local effect, faction-wide, at about one third of the level-2 value; plus that guild's per-turn limit x1.5 | fixed |

Basis:

- **Costs** scale CA's CHD factory garrison (1500 / 2500 / 5000 gold, 2 / 4 / 6 turns,
  settlement 1 / 2 / 3).
- **Reputation** is sized against the per-turn limits of 40-80: ten level-0 halls roughly fill
  the Brass Tablets' 40, so hall spam meets the limit the mod already enforces. **The
  Daemonsmiths have no limit**, so their halls pay uncapped. Watch it first.
- **The Seat's turn-limit x1.5** is a script term in `GG.grant`'s cap read, not an effect.
- **Presets**: Easy, Default, Hard and Cutthroat scale `hall_rep` as they scale the other earn
  rates.

## 6. AI

- One `cai_construction_system_building_values` row per chain. Each is cloned from CA's row
  for a vanilla chain of the same role in the same race:
  - an economy chain for the merchants;
  - a military chain for the soldiers and raiders;
  - an industry or research chain for the scholars and builders;
  - an agent chain for the spies.

  The generator picks and prints the donor rows by rule; this spec does not name group keys
  it has not read. Without a row the AI never builds the chain (memory
  `wh3-ai-construction-needs-building-values-row`: all 2,283 vanilla rows name a building, none
  is a catch-all).
- `per_faction_building_limit_*` is set, so the AI does not fill every settlement.
- No `cai_construction_system_synergies` rows. A cloned synergy row once cut a whole chain by
  75%.
- The AI chooses its guild through §4.1. It can only score what it may build. It earns, saves
  on services through `GGAI`'s use of `GG.service_cost`, and wears the Seat bundle under the
  same rules as a player.

## 7. UI and MCT

- **Construction panel: CA's own.** A hall is an ordinary building card. A locked level shows
  CA's restricted icon with §4.1's tooltip, and the card carries the existing "pays {guild}"
  line.
- **Guilds tab:**
  - `gg_rank_stats` gains the hall count ("Halls 3"), checked for width against every race's
    header by `preview_guilds_panel.py`.
  - The "Reputation this turn" ledger gains the `halls` source.
  - The price tooltip gains §4.4's line.
- **Leaderboard**: the leader's hover says whether they hold the Seat.
- **Help**: one new chapter per race, within the 21-line limit `check()` enforces.
- **Promotion message**: the rank-up feed text gains "You may now raise a {level 0 name}" (or
  the level-1 name). No new feed index.
- **No new `.twui.xml`.**
- **MCT**:
  - The switch `guild_halls` is on by default and locked in a running campaign. Off shuts
    every level, and halls already built stay but do nothing.
  - The two sliders from §5.
  - All of it is frozen into `derpy_gg_tuned` by the existing path (host's values in
    multiplayer).

`gen_great_guilds.py --check` already refuses:

- a value more than 1.5x the largest vanilla magnitude for that effect and scope, across every

- a sign that fights `is_positive_value_good` (the construction cost is a cost: -15 is the

- a scope vanilla never pairs with the effect.

exactly 1.5x. The check decides both. A value it refuses is lowered to the largest it accepts,
not argued with.

The Chaos Dwarf picks need only `TW_WH3_CHAOS_DWARFS`, which a Chaos Dwarf player owns.

**How.** The `conditions` column of `building_units_allowed` is marked unused in RPFM's
schema, so a row cannot carry the condition. Instead, the hall level lists **both** units:

- **A non-owner:** the engine's ownership gating hides the DLC unit, which leaves the fallback.
  This is the gating CA relies on in its own shared chains: the Empire's level-0 chains list
  `wh3_dlc25_*` and `wh3_dlc29_*` units for every player.
- **An owner** sees both. The fallback is a unit they can already recruit in vanilla
  buildings, so that costs nothing. Restricting it by script
  (`add_event_restricted_unit_record_for_faction`) is ruled out, because the restriction is
  per faction, not per building, and would remove it from their vanilla buildings too.

The generator derives each pick's products from the three tables above, requires a fallback
for any pick whose products are not the race's base, and requires each fallback to be base
content on that race's vanilla roster. It gets both `XP` values from the level.

## 9. Testing

Each test is watched failing before it counts (memory `test-must-measure-not-restate`).

- **`gen_great_guilds.py --check`**, new rules:
  - every race's four placement tables plus the superchain's `building_instances` row;
  - every level has its upgrade edge, card line, building value, loc and tooltip key;
  - every unit is on that race's vanilla roster and passes the DLC rule;
  - the Lua `GG.HALLS` mirrors the generator's `HALLS` (the pattern of

  `--selftest` breaks each rule once.
- **`_guilds_harness.lua`**, new blocks:
  - locks at every rank, on promotion and demotion, and on the lead changing hands both ways;

  - reputation through the turn limit;
  - the discount clamp;
  - the Seat bundle on, then off on lost lead and on lost settlement;
  - `guild_halls` off;
  - a GG_DUMP header showing "Halls 3" for all eight races.
- **`mutate_guilds.py`**: about twelve mutants, among them:

## 10. In-game checks (Chaos Dwarfs, before any other race is built)

1. A second guild's hall cannot be started in a settlement that has one: `building_instances`
   = 1 on the superchain. Inferred from 120 vanilla superchain rows and the yin/yang walls,
   not watched.
2. A locked level shows CA's restricted icon with our tooltip, and stays locked after a
   reload.
3. `faction_unique` refuses a second level-1 hall of the same guild.
4. `region:building_exists` reports a hall that is still under construction, or does not.
   This decides whether a hall pays from the turn it is ordered.
5. The AI builds halls, given a value row and an open lock.
6. The hall's local effect shows on the settlement and province with the scopes chosen.
7. A hall unit can be recruited in the hall's settlement at the expected rank.
8. A DLC unit on a modded hall is hidden from a player without the DLC, leaving the fallback
   (§8.2). If it is not hidden, the fallback is a script restriction on the DLC unit for that
   faction, using `cm:faction_has_dlc_or_is_ai(product, faction)`, the call CA's own Monster
   Hunts use.

A failure of 1 is the expensive one. The fallback is
`cm:add_event_restricted_building_chain_record_for_region`: lock the other five chains in a
region once one is built.

## 11. Later, not now

- **Switching a hall to another guild.** Cross-chain upgrade edges exist in vanilla (35
  levels, such as yin to yang). That would let a Lodge be re-pledged without demolition.
- **A one-shot effect** when a Seat is raised.
- **Halls for a ninth race**, if the lore rule ever admits one.
